# 🤖 Artificial Intelligence and Its Role in Supporting Small Projects

## Overview

This project is a graduation research project that explores the role of **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** in supporting the growth and success of small businesses. It discusses AI concepts, applications, challenges, and its impact on improving business efficiency, productivity, and decision-making while highlighting its importance in achieving **Saudi Vision 2030**.

## Objectives

* Introduce the concept of Artificial Intelligence.
* Explain the importance and applications of AI.
* Discuss the challenges and limitations of AI.
* Explore the role of AI in supporting small businesses.
* Highlight the contribution of AI to Saudi Vision 2030.

## Topics Covered

* Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
* Types of Artificial Intelligence
* Machine Learning
* Natural Language Processing (NLP)
* Large Language Models (LLMs)
* AI Applications
* AI Challenges and Ethical Issues
* Small Business Concepts
* Challenges Facing Small Businesses
* AI in Saudi Arabia
* Saudi Vision 2030

## Key Findings

* Artificial Intelligence improves operational efficiency and decision-making.
* AI helps automate repetitive business tasks.
* Small businesses can benefit from AI to increase productivity and competitiveness.
* Government initiatives in Saudi Arabia are accelerating AI adoption across multiple sectors.
